2-2 Forms of Energy
- Energy exists in different forms (thermal, mechanical, kinetic, potential,
electric, chemical, nuclear).
- Total energy is the sum of all forms of energy.
- Macroscopic forms of energy: a system holds as a whole with respect to
some outside reference frame. (e.g. KE & PE)
- Microscopic forms of energy: related to the molecular structure of a system
and the degree of the molecular activity and they are independent of
outside reference frames.
 Main forms of energy:
1) Internal energy, U: the sum of all microscopic forms of energy of a
system. It is associated with the structure and activities of the system
molecules.
2) Kinetic energy, KE: energy resulted from the system motion.
